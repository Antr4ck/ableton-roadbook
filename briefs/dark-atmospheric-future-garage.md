# Dark atmospheric future garage

## Brief de production

Dark atmospheric future garage, restrained cinematic strings, classical minor voicings, rain-like granular texture, ominous drones and floating synth pads; shuffled airy hats, ghost rimshots, muted punchy kicks and rounded mono sub in a sparse broken two-step groove. Spacious mix, clean low mids, defined transients, centered kick and snare, wide pads, filtered reverbs, light compression, natural dynamics and subtle sidechain. Instrumental; long beatless opening, gradual build, midpoint ambient pad-and-drone passage, gentle rebuild and fade to unresolved drones at 130 BPM half-time. Keep the opening extended and beatless; let the two-step groove emerge gradually, then dissolve drums and bass around the midpoint before a delicate rebuild and unresolved drone fade.
